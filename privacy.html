Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AniHancer8 enhances video on your device, uses browser permissions, stores settings, and handles diagnostics and support.

AniHancer8 v1.9.5 · Effective November 21, 2026

Local video processing

AniHancer8 uses WebGL and your browser’s GPU to enhance supported HTML5 video. Video pixels and source information are used locally to render frames and detect player changes. The extension has no remote enhancement service and does not upload video frames, source URLs, or screenshots to the developer for enhancement.

Accounts and automatic collection

The extension has no AniHancer8 account system, paid subscriptions, or user-profile backend. It contains no analytics SDK, advertising tracker, or developer data-collection endpoint. The developer does not sell locally handled video, browsing information, or saved settings.

Browser extension storage

Extension-local storage holds enhancement and color settings, profiles, favorites, appearance presets, shortcuts, and tutorial choices. Enabled-site lists and site profiles contain website hostnames. The extension uses a tab identifier to return to the selected player and uses browser-session storage for temporary per-tab overrides. AniHancer8 does not sync these values or automatically send them to the developer. You can remove saved site profiles and reset settings in A8 LAB.

Page and frame access

The extension requests access to HTTP and HTTPS sites and permission to run bundled scripts. This lets it find HTML5 players, including players embedded from other domains, and provide local playback controls. Lightweight discovery can run in accessible matching frames. Enhancement starts for eligible video on enabled sites. The extension does not request the browser history permission or use this access to collect passwords, cookies, or form contents.

Local device and playback measurements

Smart Advisor and performance tools can use browser-provided information such as CPU concurrency, approximate memory, GPU capabilities, display size, and battery status when available. They can also use video dimensions, timing, and frame counts. Optional Scene Awareness can sample small images from the selected video on your device. These measurements help recommend settings or manage processing and are not sent automatically to AniHancer8.

Diagnostics and reports

Report a Problem previews the extension and browser versions, selected settings, and available playback details. Its preview excludes website addresses, account information, and media. The separate 30-Second Diagnostics report can include the browser user-agent string, platform, timing, video dimensions, active rendering passes, and guard state. Reports are copied or downloaded only when you choose. Review the report before sharing it.

Support

The extension’s Support page opens Gmail compose when you choose to contact the project. The official website uses an email link. The extension does not automatically attach reports or send an email. If you send a message, your email provider and the project’s support mailbox handle the address, message, and any attachments you choose to share.

Updates and uninstall

After an update, the extension can show a version-specific What’s New notice linking to its bundled News Hub. On removal, Chromium may open the public AniHancer8 uninstall-information page. The extension adds no settings, hostnames, video URLs, tab IDs, tracking identifiers, query parameters, or fragments to that URL.

Website and email requests

Opening the official website, this policy, the uninstall page, or Gmail compose makes a normal request to that service. Hosting and email providers may receive ordinary request information such as your IP address and browser headers under their own policies.

User Data and Limited Use

The Chrome Web Store lists Web history and Website content. Web history here refers to local use of the current site hostname and player context. Website content includes the accessible player state and video frames the extension processes locally. This access supports the extension’s video-enhancement and playback features.

AniHancer8’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Privacy and support questions can go to anihancer8.support@gmail.com.
